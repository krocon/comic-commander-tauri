Thousands of comics, one look

My comics live in folders – CBZ, CBR, PDF, sorted by series, some with odd names. For years a small server of mine showed them as covers in the browser.

Comic Commander is its successor as a real desktop app: it opens instantly, reads every common format and never touches your comic folders.

Marc Kronberg

Made for big collections

Books are recognised by their content, not by their extension – a CBR that is really a ZIP opens just fine.

  • CBZ
  • CBR
  • CB7
  • CBT
  • PDF
  • ZIP
  • RAR 4/5
  • 7z
  • TAR
  • Your folders stay untouchedCovers and caches live in the app's own cache, nothing is written into your comic folders.
  • Instant startThe last scan shows up at once and is refreshed in the background. An unreachable drive keeps its last state.
  • Series from ComicInfo.xmlSeries, number, year, writer and manga direction from ComicRack metadata – or from the file name.
  • CategoriesComics, manga, magazines: each with its own folder, formats and cover sizes. Choose with Ctrl/⌘ 1…9.
  • All covers at onceGenerate missing thumbnails in the background with progress, time left, pause and cancel.
  • PDF in RustCovers and pages without external programs; scanned PDFs show their embedded images directly.
  • Hostile files stay harmlessArchives and images are read with limits – no zip bombs, no zip slip, broken books just show their format.
  • Keyboard firstEvery function is an action with a shortcut, in the menu and in the command palette. Your own keys in keymap.json.
  • Natural orderVolume 2 comes before Volume 10; umlauts are found in either Unicode form.
  • Coming from comiccommander?Import your old config.json with a preview; existing thumbnails are used.

Hands on the keyboard

Mouse, trackpad and swipe work too – but everything is one key away. Ctrl on Windows and Linux, ⌘ on macOS.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Comic Commander is open source under the MIT license – read the code, change it, use it.

Where can I download it?

The first public release is still being prepared. Until then you can build the app from source with Node.js, pnpm and Rust – the steps are in the README. Releases will appear on GitHub.

Does it change my comic files?

No. Comic Commander only reads your books. Covers, scan results and unpacked pages are kept in the app's own cache folder, reading positions in its settings folder.

Which formats can it read?

CBZ, CBR (RAR 4 and 5), CB7, CBT and PDF, plus the same as plain ZIP, RAR, 7z and TAR. Pages can be JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. Encrypted books are skipped.

I used the old Comic Commander server. Can I keep my setup?

Yes. Import its config.json – you see a preview of all categories with missing folders and duplicates first. Thumbnails the old server created next to your books are used as they are.

Where do the comics come from?

From your own collection: comics you scanned yourself from books you own, DRM-free purchases or freely licensed comics. Comic Commander only shows and reads what is already on your disk – it is not meant for pirated copies and has no download function.

Are the packages signed?

Not yet. The first packages will be unsigned: on macOS open the app with right-click, Open; on Windows choose More info, Run anyway in SmartScreen.

Which languages?

English for now. German, switchable while the app runs, comes with the settings.
